### Quantium Retail Strategy & Analysis
**Description:** The goal of this project was to clean, transform, and analyze retail purchase and transaction data in python for Quantium's online Data Analytics job simulation through Forge. The datasets contained over 264,000 rows that contained outliers, miscategorized products, and outlined interesting customer segment behaviour that needed to be explored. 

**Source Data:** [Purchasing Data](https://github.com/JustinFrazier16/Portfolio-BusinessAnalyst/blob/256398062cde9e7c4418e1ae85c09c95d597f7f8/Portfolio-Projects/Quantium-Retail-Strategy%26Analysis-Project/QVI_purchase_behaviour.csv) / [Transaction Data](https://github.com/JustinFrazier16/Portfolio-BusinessAnalyst/blob/256398062cde9e7c4418e1ae85c09c95d597f7f8/Portfolio-Projects/Quantium-Retail-Strategy%26Analysis-Project/QVI_transaction_data_cleandates.csv)<br>
**Python Code:** [Quantium Python Project-Python Code](https://github.com/JustinFrazier16/Portfolio-BusinessAnalyst/blob/256398062cde9e7c4418e1ae85c09c95d597f7f8/Portfolio-Projects/Quantium-Retail-Strategy%26Analysis-Project/Quantium_task1.ipynb)<br>
**Skills:** Statistical Analysis, Data Cleaning, Data Validation, Python Data Visualization<br>
**Hard Skills:** Python, Pandas, MatPlotLib<br>
**Results:** Conducted two-sample t-tests to evaluate pricing differences across consumer segments, identifying statistically significant price variation between mainstream and premium/budget chip brands for young and middle aged singles and couples. Additionally found statistically significant uptick in sales the week up to Christmas, with the rest of the year showcasing a more stationary trend. 

### Austin Animal Shelter Exploratory Analysis
**Description:** The goal of this project was to find trends within Intake Vs. Outcome data for animal shelters in Austin, Texas that could better animal welfare and improve shelter conditions by forecasting resource/staffing demands. The dataset contained 173,812 rows that needed to be cleaned and joined in SQL, matching outcomes to their corresponding inputs. This data was visualized in Looker Studio and presented in PowerPoint.

**Source Data:** [Intake Data](https://data.austintexas.gov/Health-and-Community-Services/Austin-Animal-Center-Intakes-10-01-2013-to-05-05-2/wter-evkm/explore/query/SELECT%0A%20%20%60animal_id%60%2C%0A%20%20%60name%60%2C%0A%20%20%60datetime%60%2C%0A%20%20%60datetime2%60%2C%0A%20%20%60found_location%60%2C%0A%20%20%60intake_type%60%2C%0A%20%20%60intake_condition%60%2C%0A%20%20%60animal_type%60%2C%0A%20%20%60sex_upon_intake%60%2C%0A%20%20%60age_upon_intake%60%2C%0A%20%20%60breed%60%2C%0A%20%20%60color%60/page/aggregate) / [Outcome Data](https://data.austintexas.gov/Health-and-Community-Services/Austin-Animal-Center-Outcomes-10-01-2013-to-05-05-/9t4d-g238/data_preview)<br>
**SQL Code:** [Animal Shelter Project-SQL Code](Portfolio-Projects/Animal-Shelter-Dashboard/Animal-Shelter-Project.sql)<br>
**Dashboard:** [Amimal Shelter Project-Dashboard](https://lookerstudio.google.com/s/kNlHrK0COnc)<br>
**Presentation:** [Animal Shelter Project-Presentation](https://github.com/JustinFrazier16/Portfolio-BusinessAnalyst/blob/256398062cde9e7c4418e1ae85c09c95d597f7f8/Portfolio-Projects/Animal-Shelter-Dashboard/Animal-Shelter-Presentation.pptx)<br>
**Skills:** Business Analysis, Data Cleaning, Data Validation, SQL JOINS, Data Visualization, KPI Dashboards, Reporting<br>
**Hard Skills:** Excel, SQL, Looker Studio, PowerPoint<br>
**Results:** Adoptions mainly occur in May and June due to the high amount on cat intakes, with dog intakes and adoptions being more stationary. Shorthair mix breeds for are the most prevelant within the shelter system, making them both the most adopted and the most euthanized. Pit Bulls have the highest euthanasia rates for dogs, with 48% euthanized due to aggressive behaviour, this is in comparision to the 27% of all dog breeds that are euthanized due to agressiveness. However, pit bulls also have the highest likelihood of returning to thier owners with over 30% being returned, this is followed by 23% of german sheperds and around 20% of chihuahuas. 

### Personal Income Tracking Dashboard
**Description:** The goal of this project was to better track and manage irregular income due to variability in tips. This would allow for better financial planning and trend analysis. The project covers almost 2 years of real income data.

**Dashboard:** [Personal Income Tracking Dahsboard](https://github.com/JustinFrazier16/Portfolio-BusinessAnalyst/blob/66643f2967f3db27f27cac97254c818903f8feba/Portfolio-Projects/Personal-Income-Tracking-Dashboard/Tips%20Tracker.pdf)<br>
**Skills:** Financial Analysis, Trend Analysis, Data Visualization, KPI Dashboards<br>
**Hard Skills:** Excel, Power BI, DAX, Calculated Columns<br>
**Results:** March through June have the highest tip averages, with Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays having the highest weekly average tips. Doubling equates to the highest average hourly wage at around $23.50 per hour, followed by morning shifts at $20.80 per hour, and night shifts that average $20.60 per hour. Using this knowledge, my workload has mainly shifted towards making the most of the summer months on the weekends, and working doubles if possible. Doing this will maximize revenue for time spent, allowing for the most optimal use of time while at work.
